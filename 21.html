Paneer Tikkas

Ingredients

  • 14 oz block paneer (cut into 1" cubes)

    • Marinade:

  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Red chili powder to taste
  • Salt
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 4 tsp ginger and garlic paste
  • 2 tsp tandoori masala
  • Green chilies to taste (minced)
  • 2 tsp dhana jiru (coriander cumin powder)
  • 1 tbsp oil

Instructions

  1. Put paneer in a bowl and add lemon juice to it.
  2. Allow it to rest for 5 minutes.
  3. Mix the marinade ingreidents well in a big bowl; check for salt and other spices.
  4. Add paneer to the marinade and mix well.
  5. Let paneer marinate for 4-5 hours in refrigeration.
  6. Thread paneer on pre-soaked bamboo skewers.
  7. Broil the paneer on high for 10 minutes on 1 side and 7 minutes on the other, till they are slightly brown.
  8. Once fully cooked, remove from oven and serve with onions, lemon wedges, and mint chutney.
