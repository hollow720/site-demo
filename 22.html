Zapiekanka

  • Serves 2-3 :)

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 chopped bell pepper
  • 4-5 sliced mushrooms
  • 1/2 small chopped onions
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • Crushed red chili flakes to taste
  • 1/2 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1/2 cup pasta sauce (or as needed to lightly coat veggies)
  • Jalapeños to taste
  • 1/4 cup black olives
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 cup shredded cheese (or to taste)
  • Italian bread or baguette (thin variety)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees Fahrenheit.
  2. Heat oil in a skillet.
  3. Add bell peppers, mushrooms, and onions.
  4. Season with garlic powder, red chili flakes, and italian seasoning; mix well and cook until onions are lightly caramelized, stirring frequently.
  5. Add pasta sauce and mix well.
  6. Add jalapeños, olives, and salt to taste; mix well and turn off stove.
  7. Cut into the bread at an angle near the top with a serrated knife and make it into a boat shape; the part you remove can be used to make breadcrumbs.
  8. Mix 1/2 of the cheese into veggie mixture and quickly mix so the cheese doesn't melt.
  9. Immediately fill the bread boat with the filling and sprinkle the remaining cheese over the top.
  10. Bake in a preheated oven at 450 degrees Fahrenheit until the cheese is lightly golden and bubbly (approximately 5-8 minutes).
  11. Serve hot.
