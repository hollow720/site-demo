Malai Methi Mutter

  • Makes for 5 people :)

Ingredients

  • 2 big onions (finely chopped)
  • 1 carton heavy whipping cream
  • 4 tbsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
  • 1 big bowl of peas (boiled and drained)
  • Garam masala
  • 5-6 garlic cloves (grated or finely chopped)

Instructions

  1. Take equal amounts of oil and ghee.
  2. Saute onions and garlic; once done, add salt and garam masala.
  3. Add heavy whipping cream and wait until it thickens.
  4. Add kasuri methi and peas.
