Spanish Rice

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 2 cups long grain enriched rice
  • 1/2 large onion (chopped)
  • 1 can kidney beans (washed and drained)
  • 1 medium bell pepper (chopped)
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp oregano
  • Salt to taste
  • 8 oz can tomato sauce
  • 3 cups broth
  • Cilantro or green onions to garnish

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in nonstick pan on medium heat.
  2. Add rice and saute until rice is light golden brown.
  3. Add onion and saute 1-2 minutes.
  4. Add kidney beans, bell peppers, garlic powder, salt, oregano, tomato sauce, and broth; mix well.
  5. Allow the mixture to come to a boil, stir gently, and turn the stove down to simmer; cover the pan and cook for 15 minutes.
  6. Turn off gas and fluff up rice gently with a fork.
  7. Garnish with green onions or cilantro.
