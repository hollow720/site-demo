Khaman Dhokla

Ingredients

  • 1 cup besan (gram flour)
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 4 tbsp lemon juice
  • Chili ginger paste
  • 1 pinch hing (asafoetida)
  • 1/4 tsp baking soda (preferably Eno fruit salt)
  • Salt

Vaghar (tempering):

  • Oil
  • Til (sesame seeds)
  • Rai (mustard seeds)
  • Coconut
  • Dhania (coriander)

Instructions

  1. Add salt and chili ginger paste to besan; also add hing.
  2. Dissolve sugar in 1/2 cup water.
  3. When sugar dissolves, add that water to besan and add lemon juice.
  4. Mix in 1 direction until it becomes smooth.
  5. When water boils in cooker, add baking soda to besan, put it in thali (metal plate), and heat without the whistle for 8 mins.
  6. Cut into pieces and put the vaghar (tempering) on top.
  7. Mix well and serve hot.
