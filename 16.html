Desi Mac

Ingredients

  • 2 cups pasta
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Mustard seeds
  • Curry leaves
  • 1 tbsp garlic
  • 8 oz mushroom sliced thick
  • 1 medium onion chopped
  • Green chilies
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Red chili flakes
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 3 tbsp ketchup
  • 1/2 red and 1/2 green bell peppers
  • 1 cup chopped spinach
  • 1 tbsp vinegar white
  • Cilantro
  • 1.5 cup cheese (any, preferably cheddar)
  • Breadcrumbs for topping
  • Spray oil
  • Some butter

Instructions

  1. Heat oil; add mustard and curry leaves.
  2. Add garlic and saute for 30 seconds.
  3. Add mushrooms, sprinkle salt to dehydrate them, and keep on high while stirring continuously.
  4. Add onions and bell peppers.
  5. Add black pepper and chili flakes.
  6. Saute for 2-3 minutes.
  7. Add vinegar, ketchup, and soy sauce.
  8. Saute for 1-2 minutes.
  9. Add cilantro (chopped) and spinach (chopped).
  10. Spray oil on casserole dish.
  11. Add pasta to vegetables and mix it very well.
  12. Turn stove off and add cheese; toss fast and add to casserole dish.
  13. Add breadcrumbs and put 4 blocks of butter.
  14. 350 degrees for 10 minutes and broil for last 2 minutes.
