Vegetable Jalfrezi

  • Serves 4-5 :)

Ingredients

  • Medium pot of water for boiling
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric powder
  • 3 cups cauliflower (cut into bite-size florets)
  • 1/2 cup carrots (chopped)
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1/2 onion (medium chopped)
  • 1 tsp ginger (minced)
  • 2 tsp garlic (minced)
  • 1/2 cup (4 oz) tomato sauce
  • 1/2 bell pepper (chopped into bite-size pieces)
  • 1/2 cup frozen peas (thawed and drained)
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp whole coriander seeds (dry roasted and powdered)
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • Red chili powder, optional to taste
  • 1 small deseeded and chopped tomato
  • Lemon/lime juice to taste

Instructions

  1. Bring water to a boil in a medium pot and add turmeric and salt.
  2. Add cauliflower and carrots and boil for 3 minutes.
  3. Strain the cauliflower and drop them in a bowl of ice and water to stop further cooking.
  4. Drain and keep aside for 1-2 minutes.
  5. In a nonstick skillet or pan, heat 1 tbsp oil.
  6. Add onions and cook for 2-3 minutes until light golden.
  7. Add ginger and garlic and saute for a minute.
  8. Add tomato sauce and cook till oil separates.
  9. Add bell pepper and peas and cook for 2-3 minutes.
  10. Add cauliflower, carrots, salt, powdered coriander, garam masala, and optionally red chili powder; mix well and cook until veggies are tender but not mushy.
  11. Sprinkle a little water over the veggies occassionally to provide moisture so that the dry spices do not burn.
  12. Add tomato and mix; cook for 1-2 minutes.
  13. Sprinkle lemon juice and mix well.
  14. Serve hot.
