Sweet Corn Soup

  • Serves 4 :)

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp garlic (finely minced)
  • 1 tsp ginger (grated)
  • 1 small onion (finely chopped)
  • 1/2 cup frozen peas and carrots
  • 1/4 cup green beans (cut diagonally)
  • 1/2 cup cauliflower (chopped finely)
  • 1/4 cup celery (chopped finely)
  • 1 can (14 oz) cream style corn
  • Salt, sugar, and black pepper to taste
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 14 oz can vegetable broth
  • 2 tsp corn starch (mixed with cold water)
  • 2 green onions (chopped diagonally for garnish)

Instructions

  1. In a medium pot, add oil, ginger, and garlic when oil is cool; allow it to sizzle.
  2. Add onions and saute for 30 seconds.
  3. Add peas, carrots, green beans, celery, and cauliflower; saute for 1-2 minutes.
  4. Add cream style corn, salt, and pepper; mix well.
  5. Add vegetable broth and water; mix and bring to a boil.
  6. Allow vegetables to cook without overcooking.
  7. Mix a little cold water with corn starch and add it to the soup.
  8. Add sugar and salt to the soup if necessary.
  9. Garnish spring onions and serve hot.
