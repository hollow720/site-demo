Samosa

  • Makes 12 :)

Ingredients

    Crust:

  • 1.5 cups maida (all-purpose flour)
  • 3/4 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp ajwain (carom seeds)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1/4 cup + 2 tbsp water

    • Filling:

  • 1.5 lb potatoes (boiled, peeled, and cubed)
  • 1 tsp whole coriander seeds
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds
  • Salt to taste
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp amchur (mango powder)
  • 1/2 tsp sugar
  • 10 sprigs cilantro chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger / green chili (minced)
  • Lemon juice to taste
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1/2 tsp jeera (cumin seeds)
  • 1/8 tsp hing (asafoetida)
  • 1/2 cup frozen green peas

Instructions

  1. Soak coriander and fennel seeds in a few tablespoons of warm water and keep aside.
  2. In a bowl mix maida, salt, and ajwain.
  3. Add 2 tbsp oil and mix until it is incorporated in the maida.
  4. Add lemon juice and water; knead into a smooth, firm dough, wrap in plastic wrap, and let sit for 20 minutes.
  5. Add salt, garam masala, amchur, sugar, cilantro, ginger / chili paste, lemon juice, drained coriander, and fennel seeds to the boiled, cubed potatoes; mix well.
  6. Heat oil in a pan on medium heat.
  7. Add jeera, hing, and green peas and cook for 1-2 minutes until peas are tender.
  8. Add potato mixture and cook till mixture is heated thoroughly; keep aside to cool at room temperature.
  9. Divide dough into small portions of 6 balls.
  10. Roll out each ito thin, oval shapes.
  11. Cut the oval down the center to make 2 semi-ovals.
  12. Lift one side of the flat edge and brush plain water on it; lift the opposite side and overlap the 2 edges to form a cone shape, then press the seams gently to seal.
  13. Stuff the potato mixture cone, then brush plain water around the top edge and seal shut.
  14. Deep fry until golden.
