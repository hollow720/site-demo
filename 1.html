Oriental Stir Fried Parathas

  • Cooking time: 20 mins
  • Preparation time: 45 mins
  • Makes: 12 parathas :)

Ingredients

Dough:

  • 3 teacups plain flour (maida)
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Stuffing:

  • 2 onions slices
  • 1 teacup bean sprouts
  • 1 teacup shredded cabbage
  • 1 teacup carrots, coarsely grated
  • 1/2 tsp soya sauce
  • 1/2 tsp sugar
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp oil

Instructions

Dough Mixture:

  1. Mix plain flour, oil and salt, and add a little warm water to make soft dough.
  2. Knead the dough very well for a few minutes and leave aside for half an hour.

Stuffing:

  1. Heat the oil, add the vegetables, and cook 3-4 mins
  2. Add soya sauce, sugar, and salt. Mix well and cool.

How to Proceed

  1. Divide the dough into 12 parts and roll out very thinly.
  2. Put 2-3 tbsp of the stuffing in the center and fold from each side.
  3. Put the parathas on the griddle with folded open edges at the bottom. Cook for a few minutes, turn over on other side, and cook with until crispy.
  4. Repeat for remaining parathas.
  5. Serve hot with chili garlic sauce.
