Mutter Paneer

Ingredients

  • 500g of paneer cubes (fry in ghee or add last)
  • 2 medium-sized onions (finely chopped)
  • 2 medium-sized tomatoes
  • 1 big bowl of peas
  • Chilis
  • Ginger
  • Garlic
  • 3 tbsp Dhana Jeeru (coriander-cumin powder)
  • 3 tsp Garam Masala
  • 1.5 tsp haldi (turmeric)
  • 1 pinch cumin seeds
  • Salt

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a pan and add cumin seeds; once it splutters add onions.
  2. When it turns golden brown, add chili, garlic, ginger, and all the spices; fry for 5 more mins.
  3. When oil separates add tomatoes and stir on high heat for a few mins.
  4. After 5 minutes of simmering the mixture, add 4 small glasses of water and simmer.
  5. Add peas, mix and simmer with lid partially on for 20 mins, stir occasionally.
  6. Add paneer and let it simmer till it absorbs some masala flavors.
