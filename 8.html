Hakka Noodles

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked noodles
  • 1 large clove of garlic, chopped
  • 1/2 cup spring onion, chopped (white and green separated)
  • 1/4 cup carrots, thin strips
  • 1/4 cup baby corn, thin strips
  • 1/4 cup capsicum, thin strips
  • 1/2 cup bean sprouts
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp oil, salt

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in non-stick pan and add garlic.
  2. Add white spring onions, carots, baby corn, capsicum, bean sprouts, and salt.
  3. Saute over high flame for 3-4 mins.
  4. Add noodles, soy sauce, green spring onions, and saute for another 2 mins.
  5. Serve immediately.
