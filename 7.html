Fruit Dessert

Ingredients

  • 1 lbs cream cheese (Philadelphia)
  • 1 pack whipped topping (8 oz)
  • 1/2 can sweetened condensed milk (14 oz)
  • 1 can mixed fruits (avoid w/cherry juice and preferably in pear juice & select w/o bananas)
  • 1 can diced pineapples
  • Some fresh grapes
  • Blanched almonds, pistachios, and walnuts

Instructions

  1. In a huge vessel first blend cream cheese with a hand blender.
  2. When it becomes smooth, add whipped topping and blend.
  3. Add 1/2 can of condensed milk.
  4. Blend them all well, then add all fruits without juice.
  5. Mix well with a spoon and add the nuts.
  6. Chill and serve.
