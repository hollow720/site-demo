Vegetable Fried Rice

  • Cooking time: 30 mins
  • Makes for 4 people :)

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Chinese rice
  • .5 cup French beans (cut diagonally into long strips)
  • .5 cup carrots (cut into thin, long strips)
  • .5 cup capsicum (thin, long strips)
  • 1 tbsp celery chopped
  • 1 cup white spring onions (chopped)
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 cup green spring onions (chopped)
  • 1 tbsp oil, salt

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in pan.
  2. Add veggies and saute over a high flame for 3-4 mins until veggies soften.
  3. Add rice, soy sauce, green spring onions, and salt.
  4. Mix well and saute for 2 mins.
  5. Serve hot.
