Malai Kulfi

  • Makes 22-25 :)

Ingredients

  • 14 oz sweetened condensed milk
  • 12 oz evaporated milk
  • 16 oz heavy whipping cream
  • 16 oz cool whip topping
  • 1 big pinch kesar (saffron)
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/2 cup cashews, pistachios, and almonds (coarsely ground)

Instructions

  1. Grind together sugar and saffron with a mortar and pestle.
  2. In a large mixing bowl, combine sweetened condensed milk, evaporated milk, heavy whipping cream, and cool whip.
  3. Using a hand blender (or by hand), gently mix all ingredients together.
  4. Add sugar and saffron mixture and nuts; mix well.
  5. Pour mixture into small Dixie style plastic cups; cover cups with foil and insert a wooden popsicle stick into the center.
  6. Freeze kulfi for at least 4-6 hours, preferably overnight.
