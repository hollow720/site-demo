Chinese Rice

  • Used in Vegetable Fried Rice, the previous recipe
  • Makes 4 cups :)

Ingredients

  • 1 cup long grain rice
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp salt

Instructions

  1. Wash rice thouroughly and soak in 3 cups of water for 30 mins; drain and keep aside.
  2. Boil 6-8 cups of water, add salt and 1 tbsp oil.
  3. Add rice to boiling water; cook till rice is 85% cooked.
  4. Pour into a colander and drain; pour cold water on it.
  5. Let all water drain out ensuring that rice does not have any moisture.
  6. Add 1 tbsp oil and toss juice in it.
  7. Spread cooked rice on flat surface till it cools.
  8. Use as required.
