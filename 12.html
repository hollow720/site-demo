Manchurian Balls

  • Goes with Manchurian Gravy (#11)

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cabbage (finely chopped)
  • 1.25 cup carrots (grated)
  • 1/2 cup chopped onions
  • 2 tbsp corn flour
  • 5 tbsp maida (all-purpose flour)
  • 3-4 cloves garlic (finely chopped)
  • 1 green chili (finely chopped)
  • 1/4 tsp Ajinomoto powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Oil for deep frying

Instructions

  1. Combine the cabbage, carrots, onions, corn flour, maida, garlic, green chili, ajinomoto powder, salt, and pepper in a bowl; mix well.
  2. Shape spoonfuls of the mixture into small balls. If you find it difficult to form the balls, sprinkle a little water to bind the mixture.
  3. Deep fry in hot oil until golden brown. Drain on absorbent paper and keep aside.

To Serve:

  1. Just before serving, put the balls into the sauce and bring to a boil.
  2. Serve hot.
