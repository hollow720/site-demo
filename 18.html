Jeera Cookies

Ingredients

  • 3 parts Bisquick Powder (not less sodium)
  • 1 part maida (all-purpose flour)
  • 1 part oil
  • 1 part milk
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • Jeera (cumin seeds)

Instructions

  1. Mix everything well.
  2. Preheat oven to 225 degrees.
  3. Bake for 1 hour.
  4. Then decrease heat to 175 degrees and heat for 1 more hour or till they are hard and done.
