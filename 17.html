Chakri

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rice flour
  • 1 cup wheat atta (flour)
  • 1 cup maida (all-purpose flour)
  • Salt
  • Chili ginger paste
  • Sesame seeds
  • 1 stick butter

Instructions

  1. Melt butter and mix all together.
  2. Make medium dough (not too hard).
  3. First make chakri on foil then fry in medium hot oil.
