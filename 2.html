Kadhai Paneer

  • Makes for 8 people :)

Ingredients

  • 3 big bell peppers
  • 5 big tomatoes/1 can tomato puree (3 big spoons)
  • 4 onions
  • 11 garlic cloves
  • Salt
  • Turmeric
  • Dhana Jeeru (coriander-cumin powder)
  • Garam Masala
  • Red Masala (red chili powder)
  • 2 tsp Kasuri Methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
  • Paneer
  • Oil and Ghee

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil and ghee.
  2. Add chopped garlic and saute.
  3. Add onions and when they become soft add capsicum.
  4. Add tomatoes and saute till ghee comes on top.
  5. Add all spices.
  6. Add kasuri methi and paneer.
  7. Serve hot.
