Chinese Spring Rolls

Ingredients

Stuffing:

  • 1 cup cabbage (cut thin and long)
  • 1 cup spring onions (chopped long)
  • 1/2 cup boiled noodles (oil it and keep aside)
  • 1/2 cup bell peppers (thin and long)
  • 1 tsp pepper and salt
  • 2 tsp chili sauce
  • 1/4 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oil

Wrap:

  • 1 cup maida (all-purpose flour)
  • 1/2 cup corn flour
  • Salt
  • Some baking soda
  • Water

Instructions

Stuffing:

  1. Heat oil in a nonstick pan.
  2. Put onions and bell peppers in; stir fry for 1 minute.
  3. Then add cabbage, chili sauce, salt, and pepper and mix well.
  4. Add the boiled noodles and switch off the gas to cool it.
  5. Add 3 tbsp maida to a little water and heat it; mix it to make paste.

To Wrap:

  1. Mix maida, cornflour, salt, and baking soda and make dough like roti (flatbread).
  2. Keep aside for 10 minutes.
  3. Again knead the dough and make small balls about the size for roti.
  4. Then roll them into 5" diameter and spread on paper.
  5. Cover it with another newspaper.

To Stuff:

  1. Place 1 roti on board; put some stuffing at the bottom of the roti and roll to the top.
  2. Fold the corners with maida paste.
  3. Fry till golden brown and serve with chili sauce.
