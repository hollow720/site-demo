Paneer Mix Vegetable

Ingredients

  • 1 big onion
  • 1 small can tomato puree / 4 medium tomatoes
  • 1 big capsicum
  • 3/4 cereal bowl corn
  • 1/4 cereal bowl peas
  • 3-4 cloves garlic
  • Ginger
  • Red chili powder
  • Garam masala
  • Paneer or tofu
  • 1/4 pack heavy cream

Instructions

  1. Chop onion and pepper in small pieces.
  2. Half boil pepper, corn, and peas in salty water, and drain so they become soft and retain color.
  3. Saute paneer/tofu in ghee and keep aside.
  4. Now in a nonstick pan take oil and ghee, add ginger, garlic, and onions one by one.
  5. When they are done, add tomato puree.
  6. After the oil is separated, add vegetables and also add masala.
  7. Mix well and cook for 5-7 minutes; then add heavy cream and and cook again for 5-7 minutes.
  8. Add tofu/paneer when serving; garnish with cilantro.
