Spaghetti

Ingredients

  • 1 pack (1 lb) spaghetti (angel hair)
  • 1 bottle soy sauce
  • Ragu spaghetti sauce (traditional or garlic)
  • Sambal chili garlic paste

Vegetables:

  • Bell peppers (3 colors)
  • Onions
  • Baby corn
  • Carrots
  • Broccoli
  • Cabbage
  • Spring onions
  • Chestnuts
  • Black pepper
  • Salt
  • Jalapeños and its vinegar
  • 4-5 tbsp oil

Instructions

  1. Boil spaghetti, drain, and leave aside
  2. Take oil in a wide and big pan and heat it on a high flame; then add onions.
  3. Add soy sauce and when onions are done, add all veggies one by one.
  4. Add some salt and jalapeños.
  5. Add noodles
  6. Add spaghetti sauce, black pepper, vinegar, and chili garlic paste.
  7. Mix well and serve hot.
