Manchurian Gravy

  • Goes with Manchurian Balls (#12)

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp garlic (finely chopped)
  • 2 tsp green chillies (finely chopped)
  • 2 tsp ginger (finely chopped)
  • 1 cup vegetable stock or water
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp corn flour mixed with 1 cup water
  • 2 pinches sugar
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in nonstick pan on high flame.
  2. Add garlic, green chillies, and ginger and stir fry over high flame for a few seconds.
  3. Add water, soy sauce, corn flour paste, sugar, and salt and simmer for a few minutes.
  4. Serve hot.
